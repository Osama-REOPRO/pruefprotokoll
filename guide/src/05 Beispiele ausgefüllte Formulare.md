# Beispiele – your filled-in forms decoded

## 1 · Stahlschiebetor, Tousek PULL T8/P – „ohne Mängel“

- Steel sliding gate with Tousek PULL T8 rack drive (Tousek: max. 800 kg leaf, 230 V, self-learning end positions, 8.2 kΩ safety-edge input, lockable release).
- „230 V / 24 V“ = mains supply / control voltage.
- „Kettenrad und Kette“ crossed out → **Zahnst.** – correct, the gate is rack-driven ([[02 Tortypen#^abb-04|Abb. 4 · Schiebetor]]).
- Endschalter **DES** ticked – correct: no cams, positions are learned electronically ([[03 Formular 1 – Tore#^abb-12|Abb. 12 · NES/DES]]).
- Remark „ohne Mängel“ = no defects → all checked lines o.B.

## 2 · Feuerschutztor T90 „SG“ (Hotz) – Akkus defekt

- Hotz T90 fire gate, drive BM II 0.25 kW, 400 V / 24 V. „SG“ and „BM II“ could not be decoded – read from the plates.
- Catch device: **Sperrklinke**, GfA Gr. 1 (old size, up to ~1997) – pawl type ([[03 Formular 1 – Tore#^abb-07|Abb. 7 · Fangvorrichtung]]). If it has caught, replace the damping bracket.
- Hold-open system **FHK-I** – Hotz system for fire rolling gates (DIBt Z-6.5-1541, listed as expired: normal for existing installations, but use only parts the approval allows).
- Triggers **2 × ORM 130 A** = Hekatron optical smoke detectors, obsolete series (resellers name approved RetroFit successors) + **HAT 2 Stück** = 2 manual release buttons ([[02 Tortypen#^abb-05|Abb. 5 · FS-Rolltor]]).
- Battery Yuasa 12 V 17 Ah ticked **B.** → „Akkus müssen getauscht werden“ – urgent: the gate depends on it to close during a power cut.

## 3 · Sektionaltor Hörmann SPU, WA 400 FU – Angebot

- Hörmann SPU (F42) industrial sectional door, double-walled PU-foam panels; shaft drive WA 400 FU (FU = frequency converter, soft start/stop) – [[02 Tortypen#^abb-02|Abb. 2 · Sektionaltor]].
- B. at **Seile** → „Seil rechts muss getauscht werden Ø 4 mm ca. 5 m“ – right lifting cable. Check drum and cable-break device; replace in pairs if the manufacturer says so ([[03 Formular 1 – Tore#^abb-09|Abb. 9 · Seile]]).
- B. at **Kunststoffeinlagen / Dichtungen** → „Bodendichtung defekt“.
- „Angebot“ at the top + „Bitte um Angebot“ at the bottom = customer requests a quote for the repair.
