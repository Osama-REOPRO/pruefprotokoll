# Hotz Feldhandbuch Tore & Türen

Field reference guide to the two Hotz Torservice inspection forms – every line translated (DE → EN), explained and illustrated.

- **Formular 1** „Prüfprotokoll für kraftbetätigte Tore gemäß ASR A1.7 und DIN 14677“ – power-operated gates.
- **Formular 2** „Abnahme-/Inbetriebnahme- & Prüfprotokoll“ – mainly fire and smoke doors.

1. [[01 Grundlagen]] – o.B. / B. / n.v., safety before work, rules, intervals, force limits
2. [[02 Tortypen]] – rolling, sectional, sliding and fire gates with labelled drawings
3. [[03 Formular 1 – Tore]] – every item of Form 1
4. [[04 Formular 2 – Türen]] – every item of Form 2
5. [[05 Beispiele ausgefüllte Formulare]] – three real filled-in forms decoded

7. [[07 Glossar]] – abbreviations and jargon
8. [[08 Handbücher und Links]] – manufacturer manuals with real photos
